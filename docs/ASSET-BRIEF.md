# Visual Asset Brief

The site is Vedat Erdem Özkul's visual CV. The trivago influence belongs in the visual language only: bold color fields, confident black outlines, friendly editorial illustration, simple compositions, and expressive but uncluttered characters. Do not copy an existing trivago illustration or use the trivago logo inside the artwork.

## Integrated: 3 custom PNG illustrations

All three assets are generated and live in `assets/illustrations/`. Solid section-matched backgrounds were selected for more reliable edges and faster rendering than masked transparency.

### 1. Hero portrait — `vedat-data-portrait.png`

- Placement: hero, beside the name and professional profile
- Final canvas: 1024 × 1536 px, yellow background
- Subject: a friendly illustrated likeness of Vedat seated or standing with a laptop; a few floating data cards, a small chart, and connected records around him
- Composition: full or three-quarter body, weighted toward the center/right, generous clear space around the silhouette
- Style: hand-drawn editorial character, crisp black outline, flat fills, subtle irregularity
- Palette: black, white, yellow `#FFCC32`, sky blue `#6BD0F6`, pink `#EF3B67`, orange `#FF8538`
- Avoid: logos, readable interface text, photorealism, gradients, 3D, corporate stock-art look
- Source needed from Vedat: one clear front-facing or three-quarter portrait photo with good light

### 2. Data processing — `data-processing-pipeline.png`

- Placement: AI-powered language dataset case study
- Final canvas: 1774 × 887 px, yellow background
- Subject: messy vocabulary cards entering a playful processing machine or workflow; duplicate/missing records are flagged; clean linked records and a small chart emerge
- Message: exploration, cleaning, enrichment, validation, and human oversight
- Style and palette: same as the hero illustration
- Avoid: robots replacing people, magic-wand metaphors, readable tiny text, logos

### 3. Business transformation — `business-data-network.png`

- Placement: 120+ reports / 287-dealer transformation case study
- Final canvas: 1536 × 1024 px, sky-blue background
- Subject: a central dashboard connecting multiple business functions and a distributed network of dealer nodes; one person translates the signal into a decision
- Message: fragmented systems becoming a coherent operating view
- Style and palette: same as the hero illustration
- Avoid: literal maps of Türkiye, brand logos, unreadable dashboards, excessive node density

## Optional real assets

- One high-quality portrait photo for the contact/about area: vertical 1600 × 2000 px, neutral background, natural expression.
- One architecture project image and one anonymized dashboard screenshot. Use only if sharing rights and confidentiality allow.

## Final export rules

- PNG, optimized by the browser at responsive display sizes
- sRGB
- No embedded text, logos, or watermarks
- Full-resolution originals retained; responsive sizing is handled in CSS
